How do I play?

Start at a circle and connect neighboring tiles horizontally or vertically. Draw with your finger or tap one tile at a time. Fill every tile. If a board has a finish, reach it last. Lifting your finger keeps your path.

What do the numbers and extra circles mean?

A tile marked 2 needs two visits; a tile marked 3 needs three. Go around another route to return. If there are several starting circles, start a line from each unused circle. Already filled tiles stay filled.

Can I correct a move?

Drag backward from the end of your line to erase a step. Going straight back erases a visit; it does not count as another visit. Use Hint if you get stuck, or Restart level to clear the current path. A hint may rewind part of a blocked route.

How do I open a chapter?

Chapter 1 is free. Watch a rewarded ad to unlock each later chapter, with 10 levels per chapter. Complete the previous level to continue within an open chapter. Ads need internet; unlocked puzzles work offline.

The ad will not load, or I closed it early.

If no ad is available or you close it before earning a reward, the chapter stays locked. Check your connection and try again. If an ad is still unavailable, try later. Your puzzle progress stays saved.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress, unlocked chapters, colors and settings are stored on this device. There is no account or cloud sync between devices. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove them. Device backups may restore data, depending on your operating system and settings. The old One Line save format cannot be imported.

How do I get more line colors?

Finish a chapter to earn its color. Open Line color from the home screen and select an unlocked color. Earned colors and ad-unlocked chapters remain after Reset progress.

How do I reset or delete my data?

Reset progress in Settings clears puzzle progress, milestones, the current path and recovery copies. Settings, earned colors and unlocked chapters remain. Uninstall the app or use your device’s app-data controls to remove the remaining data. Manage device backups separately.

Sound, vibration or animation settings?

Open Settings to adjust Sound effects, Vibration and Reduce motion. Also check device volume, silent mode and connected headphones. Vibration support varies by device. You can choose Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese or Spanish in Language.

What should I include in a bug report?

Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, level number and the steps that caused the problem. An error screenshot can help. Please remove personal information and do not send passwords or payment details.
