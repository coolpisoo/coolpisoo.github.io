Privacy policy

This policy describes how coolpisoo handles information in One Line (listed on the App Store as One Line: Calm Puzzle) for Android and iPhone, and on these support pages.

Effective September 15, 2026 · coolpisoo

Your game stays on your deviceAdvertisingStorage and deletionSupport correspondence

Your game stays on your device

The app stores your completed levels, current path, hint and undo counts, achievements, colors, chapter unlocks and preferences on your device. It may keep a local recovery copy if a save cannot be read. These game records are not sent to a developer-operated server. The app has no account, payment system or developer-operated cloud save.

Advertising

Chapter 1 is free. Later chapters open after you choose to complete a Google AdMob rewarded ad. Ads require an internet connection. The app requests non-personalized ads; this still involves data processing. Google’s advertising SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Google may process this data on servers outside your country. Consent and ad privacy choices are offered where required and available through Google’s consent system. Where available, open Ad privacy settings in the app’s Settings to review your choices.

Google Privacy Policy · Google Mobile Ads · iOS · Google Mobile Ads · Android

Storage and deletion

Local game records remain until you reset them or remove the app’s data. Reset progress deletes puzzle progress, milestones, the current path and recovery copies, while keeping preferences, earned colors and unlocked chapters. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove all local records. Operating-system backups are separate and may restore app data; manage or delete them through your device or backup provider. We cannot access or delete records stored only on your device. Google’s retention and deletion practices follow its privacy policy.

Support correspondence

If you email coolpisoo@gmail.com, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request and resolve the reported issue. Correspondence is kept only as long as needed for that purpose and follow-up, unless a longer period is required by law. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing the same address. Do not include passwords, payment details or unrelated personal files.

These web pages

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising scripts, analytics, cookies or contact forms. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and request logs to provide and secure its hosting service, under its own privacy statement. Email links open your email app; no message is sent until you choose to send it.

GitHub Privacy Statement

Other providers and privacy requests

Android, iOS and the app stores handle their own backups, store records and permitted diagnostics under their policies. To exercise privacy rights or raise a concern, including information sent by a child, contact coolpisoo at the address below. Requests concerning data processed by Google or GitHub may need to be addressed to that provider.

Policy updates

We update this page and its effective date when our information-handling practices change. Material changes will be explained here and, where required, in the app.
